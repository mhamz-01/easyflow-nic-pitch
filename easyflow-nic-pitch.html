Incubation pitch for NIC Peshawar

The problem

Small teams have no single, easy-to-adopt platform for the essentials of running a project.

What a small team pieces together today

  • Tasks and boardsJira
  • DocumentationGoogle Docs
  • WhiteboardsMiro, Excalidraw
  • Chat and meetingsSlack, Discord

Each tool is strong on its own. Spread across four apps, the team loses a single view of project progress, and communication falls behind.

What that costs the team

  • 1–2 wks

    Learning curve

    to get comfortable with each tool, then train the whole team

  • 51 min

    Switching

    lost per worker each week to tool fatigue

  • ~$33

    Cost

    per user each month for four paid plans

Sources: Jira user reviews (Capterra, AWS Marketplace); Lokalise tool fatigue survey of 1,000 workers (2025); vendor list prices (2026). Learning time is user-reported.

Our solution

Every essential feature in one simple platform, with fewer clicks and more on each screen.

  • Less learning

    One interface to learn instead of four.

  • No switching

    Tasks, docs, whiteboards, chat, and meetings together.

  • Active communication

    Project channels and meetings with AI summaries.

  • AI-paced

    Ask Brain to create tasks or report, in one prompt.

Does it answer a real need? Yes.

In service and product agencies, time is money and outcomes are checked, so they need tools that are quick and clear.
